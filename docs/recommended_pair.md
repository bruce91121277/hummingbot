# Recommended pair and integration priority

**Continue TxFlow x Lighter Core as the first public-data research route, but classify it as a capture candidate rather than a strategy with demonstrated edge.** TxFlow is the only screened new platform with a live, documented BTC-USDC perpetual, a documented full-snapshot public book feed, published fees/contract units, active points, and paired Oracle captures already verified by manifest. That makes it the most testable route now. Its 4.5 bps VIP0 taker fee and sparse/stale observed feed are meaningful drawbacks.

The Oct 29, 2026 Oracle captures total 360 seconds and 6,557 market events: TxFlow 298 (0.83 events/s) and Lighter Core 6,259 (17.39 events/s). At the 500 ms gate, 4 hypothetical entries were modeled; 2 timed out and 2 remained censored at capture end. Neither closed trade exited because of basis convergence. Under TxFlow 4.5 bps taker / Lighter zero-fee assumptions, the two timeout net results were -0.082426 and -0.103808 quote units; the two censored entries each incurred 0.045 assumed quote units of entry fees, with exit cost unknown. Under zero fees, the two timeout gross results still sum to -0.006274 quote units. These amounts are hypothetical model units, not verified USD PnL, because the Lighter contract multiplier, settlement currency and conversion are unverified.

The zero-fee pair comparison on a $100,000-per-leg scenario is: TxFlow x Lighter Core 9 bps / $90 all-taker round trip, RISEx x Lighter Core 6 bps / $60, and Nado x Lighter Core 7 bps / $70. The latter routes assume zero Lighter fees and omit currency conversion, funding, spread/slippage and depth; they are not observed execution costs. RISEx has the lowest published base taker fee among these two-venue candidates, but lacks a local paired capture. Nado is a credible follow-up fee/reward candidate, but market data cadence and compatible contract economics are also untested.

Next evidence to collect: at least several independent multi-hour TxFlow/Lighter captures across sessions, validate source/receive clock alignment and Lighter fee/contract metadata, and measure how often synchronized executable quotes survive the freshness gate. If TxFlow remains below roughly one book update per second or most synchronized windows fail, pause its strategy work and capture RISEx or Nado before further engine changes. Do not assign a cash value to points.

**Conclusion:** no route currently has sufficient evidence of positive net convergence returns. TxFlow is recommended because it is the best-evidenced route to test next, not because the current replay is profitable.
