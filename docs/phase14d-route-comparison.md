# Phase 14D: hypothetical route comparison

Both routes use the same independent replay engine: 100 assumed quote units per left entry, 10 bps BBO trigger, 25% mid-basis convergence exit, 120 s maximum holding, 100 ms hedge delay, full visible depth and 500 ms freshness. Four-leg fee assumptions: Extended 2 bps / Core 0 bps; Arcus 2.25 bps / RH 0 bps. These are **scenarios**, not actual account fees. Funding is assumed zero. One-second observations unless noted. Source clocks, contract economics, the 1:1 hedge and quote conversion are assumptions, and results are hypothetical, not verified USD PnL. Captures are distinct and routes and dates are not matched experiments.

The manifest check accepted 14 captures and excluded 2; only 7 had usable books from both venues for each route. A valid sampled pair is not an independent time window or signal.

At 100 ms, the Extended convergence trade lost -0.004753 after fees, and all three Arcus convergence trades lost money after fees. Repeated use of a single quote inflates raw sample counts: at 100 ms, Extended had 7,669 valid samples but 6,636 distinct retained quote pairs; Arcus had 21,546 samples but 15,932 distinct retained quote pairs. The 1-second Extended baseline had 760 unique pairs. Distinct pairs do not imply independent signals. With 100 ms stored-book sampling, the replay may omit intermediate original events, so it does not establish real executable sub-second fills. Gaps hide crossings and risk between observed samples.

As a chronological exploratory split, 2026-10-27 is exploration and 2026-10-28 is a provisional holdout (selected after capture collection, not a pre-registered locked holdout). For the 500 ms / 1000 ms-decision fee scenario, Extended holdout had 7 entries, 2 timeout closes totaling -0.070049 and 5 open; Arcus holdout had 2 entries, both open and no closed PnL. Neither validates positive net returns. No cross-capture continuation is assumed.

## Quality and provenance

Extended source `ts` is epoch milliseconds generated by the system, according to [Extended public order-book documentation](https://api.docs.extended.exchange/). Snapshot absolute size uses `q`, delta absolute size uses `c`; the adapter conservatively rejects gaps in consecutive sequence numbers until a new snapshot, although strict +1 continuity is not established by that documentation. Lighter Core captured `last_updated_at` is interpreted as microseconds based on capture metadata and magnitude; the unit and synchronization are UNVERIFIED. Lighter Core and RH use separate files and discovery identifiers. No conclusion constitutes formal book, time, economic-unit, or execution validation.

Decision rule: classify a route RESEARCH_INSUFFICIENT if clock or economic units are unverified, most positions are censored, or too few independent opportunities exist; use LOW_PRIORITY if it repeatedly fails net-cost thresholds after these inputs are validated. Both routes are RESEARCH_INSUFFICIENT. Temporarily lower priority for Arcus/RH relative to repairing capture quality, not because profit is disproven. Extended/Core merits a longer independent capture first to investigate its observable BTC spreads, provided timestamps, book validity, and economic equivalence are validated. Neither route has sufficient evidence for positive net returns.

Warnings in the ARM64 test environment: pytest does not recognize `asyncio_default_fixture_loop_scope`, `timeout`, and `timeout_method`, because pytest-asyncio and pytest-timeout are not installed in the isolated research environment; no plugins were installed or global environment changed. Research suite plus selected upstream order book tests passed; the complete upstream suite was not run.

Open limitations: source clock synchronization, source-economic unit binding, independent quote-pair and independent-signal confidence, missing exact maximum adverse excursion including gaps, maker/funding settlement, original captures' limited duration, and a future locked holdout. The fixed 100 ms book retention may omit intermediate events, and gaps leave unknown exposure. Emergency liquidation is a counterfactual and does not reconcile partial hedge inventory or create a fill.
